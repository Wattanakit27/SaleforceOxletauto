# Automotive Dealer CRM and Lead-Handling Best Practices (Global and Thailand/SEA, as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: These notes cover dealer-specific CRM products and measured lead-handling practice. Most hard data is from the US, where vendors and mystery-shop firms publish benchmarks. Many sources are vendors that sell lead-response, BDC, or AI tools, and they are flagged where it matters. Older statistics are dated inline.

---

## Q1. Which dealer CRMs dominate (US/EU/Asia), what are their key features, and what do they cost?

### Takeaway
The US franchise market runs on a few DMS-linked platforms: VinSolutions (Cox), CDK CRM/Elead, DealerSocket (Solera), DriveCentric, Tekion and Reynolds. Typical cost is roughly USD 500–3,000 per rooftop per month, quote-only. In Europe/UK, Keyloop (lead aggregation plus showroom tools) and DMS-embedded CRM such as Pinewood lead. Salesforce Automotive Cloud and Dynamics 365 partner builds are aimed at OEMs and large groups. Asia has no dominant dealer-CRM brand: OEM networks use enterprise CRMs (e.g., LeadSquared in India), and Thai used-car lots pair a simple stock/DMS program with a LINE/Facebook chat inbox.

### Cited Findings
**Market size / structure (US)**
- The US has about 18,000 franchise dealerships and 40,000 independent dealers. One market-research figure puts the automotive CRM market at USD 6.79B in 2025, projected to reach USD 9.58B by 2029. This is a vendor blog citing market research, so treat it as low reliability. — [ringlead.ca](https://www.ringlead.ca/blog/compare/best-automotive-crm-compared-2026/)

Strengths: deep desking and reporting, multi-store login, and shopper behavioral data from Cox properties (Autotrader, KBB). Criticized for a dated UI and duplicate-lead handling. ### Inferences
- Dealer CRMs differ from general CRMs mainly through: (a) tight DMS/inventory linkage (VIN-level stock, desking, deal structures); (b) lead ingestion from many third-party listing sites; (c) built-in multichannel texting and video; (d) equity mining and service-to-sales; (e) OEM-compliant reporting. For a Thai used-car lot, (a), (b), (c) and want-list matching matter most. OEM reporting and lease desking are irrelevant.
- Pricing benchmarks (USD 400–3,000/rooftop/month) suggest that an in-house build is economically rational for a single Thai used-car lot. The comparison should focus on features and process, not on buying these products, none of which localize to LINE or Thai finance.

### Gaps
- No verified per-vendor market share for 2025/2026. I found no independent source for Reynolds and Reynolds CRM features or pricing.
- Ownership history not verified this session (from background knowledge only, unconfirmed): CDK's acquisition of Elead (c. 2018), Cox's acquisition of VinSolutions (c. 2011), Keyloop's origin as CDK Global's international business (c. 2020), and Pinewood's separation from Pendragon after Lithia's 2024 purchase of Pendragon's UK retail business.
- Keyloop pricing, Pinewood lead-routing details and Motortech.ai deal completion status are unverified.

## Q2. How do dealer workflows work (lead sources, BDC, routing, appointments, test drive, trade-in, F&I, delivery, service retention)?

### Takeaway
The canonical flow is: capture lead from website, third-party listings, phone, chat, social or walk-in → log in CRM (often missed) → fast multichannel first response by BDC or salesperson → appointment → show → test drive and trade-in appraisal → desk/F&I → delivery → service and equity-mining loop. The measurable leaks are unlogged leads, slow or no response, weak human handoffs after automation, and low appointment show rates. Buyers want more of the process (credit application, price, F&I) done online than dealers currently allow.

### Cited Findings
**Lead sources and shopper behavior**
- In Cox Automotive's 16th Car Buyer Journey Study, 77% of used buyers used third-party websites, 60% dealer websites, 41% search engines, 34% used-online-retailer sites, 26% social media and 11% AI sites. Used buyers visited an average of 4.8 site types (4.0 for new). Survey of 2,300 buyers, fall 2025; released Jan 13, 2026. — [Cox Automotive](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/cox-automotive-car-buyer-journey-study-finds-efficiency-digital-tools-and-ai-drive-record-satisfaction/)
- In the same study, 53% completed all steps in person and 7% bought entirely online. Gaps between what buyers want online and what they actually do: credit application 48% vs 33%, F&I products 40% vs 16%, finalizing price 37% vs 19%. 63% prefer omnichannel. "Mostly digital" buyers were the most satisfied. 75% of used buyers were highly satisfied. — [Cox Automotive](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/cox-automotive-car-buyer-journey-study-finds-efficiency-digital-tools-and-ai-drive-record-satisfaction/)
- In the 2024 study, buyers who did key steps online (credit app, paperwork) saved about 42 minutes at the dealership (used buyers: 40 minutes). — [search summary of Cox 2024 study](https://b2b.autotrader.com/resources/top-takeaways-car-buyer-journey/)
- Calls generated the most total leads, but web forms produced the highest share of buyers. Paid channels had the highest share of sales leads and, for the first time, the highest buy rate. Covers 22,900+ dealer websites and 1.4B visits in 2025. — [Foureyes 2026 Benchmarks (Apr 22, 2026)](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/2026-automotive-dealer-benchmarks-report)
- About 40% of internet leads arrive when the dealership is closed (DAS mystery shop of 1,500+ US dealers, Jan 2018). — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-dealership-online-lead-response-time-often-falls-short/)
- 44% of buyers research more before contacting a dealer, and 59% would drive further for a better price (Urban Science/Harris Poll, 3,012 US buyers, Jan 2026). — [Urban Science](https://www.urbanscience.com/resources/urban-science-harris-poll-2026-auto-buying-digital-fragmented/)

**Lead capture/logging discipline**
- 15.2% of qualified leads were never logged in the CRM in 2025, rising every year from 10.6% in 2020. 42.7% of qualified leads were "mishandled." Website phone calls were 26.3% of unlogged leads (2025 report). — [Foureyes 2026](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/2026-automotive-dealer-benchmarks-report); [Foureyes 2025](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/2025-automotive-dealer-benchmarks-report)

**BDC model and appointment funnel**
- Foureyes Q1 2026 (2.6M leads, 1,150+ dealers, 48 US markets): contact rate 69% (range 47–79%), appointment set rate 40% (30–59%), show rate 59% (41–74%), overall lead close rate 18.5%. Best market (Des Moines): 69% show, 59% show-to-sale, 30.1% close. Reported via Spyne, a vendor. — [Spyne citing Foureyes](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/dealership-ai-benchmarks)
- Show-rate targets vary by vendor: "60–75% industry target, 80%+ excellent"; "50–65%, 70%+ excellent"; sales 60–70% vs service 75–85%. — [spyne BDC metrics](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/automotive-bdc-metrics); [useflai](https://www.useflai.com/blog/bdc-metrics-every-dealership-should-track); [Proactive Training Solutions](https://proactivetrainingsolutions.com/dealership-appointment-set-rates-2026-benchmarks-and-fixes/)
- Service no-shows are about 20% (Solera), or 20–24% for no-shows plus cancellations (Keyloop, May 2026). Maritz warns that aggressive set-rate targets create "soft" appointments. — [Spyne citing vendors](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/dealership-ai-benchmarks)

**Lead routing / ownership**
- Salesforce Automotive Cloud routes high-value leads to the right dealer with history attached. CDK CRM (Elead) offers lead routing plus escalation rules. Keyloop Leads adds alerts and ongoing response reminders. — [Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/automotive-cloud-foundations/drive-stakeholder-collaboration); [Kimoby](https://www.kimoby.com/blog/crm-car-dealership); [newswire.ca](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/keyloop-acquires-rapid-rtc-and-enquirymax-885863074.html)
- Situations needing a human scored 9 points lower on average, and customers were twice as likely to get no personal response. "The greatest risk is in the handoffs between systems and from AI to dealership staff" (Cameron O'Hagan, Pied Piper). CRM dashboards can show activity even when the customer experience failed. — [Auto Remarketing on Pied Piper 2026](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-finds-ai-raises-dealerships-internet-lead-responsiveness-but-creates-added-risks)

**Test drive, trade-in, F&I, delivery**
- Salesforce Automotive Cloud supports test-drive scheduling on leads and opportunities. — [Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/automotive-cloud-foundations/drive-stakeholder-collaboration)
- enquiryMAX (Keyloop) covers capture → building the deal → presenting the offer → vehicle handover. — [newswire.ca](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/keyloop-acquires-rapid-rtc-and-enquirymax-885863074.html)
- ProMax matches customer credit to lenders and vehicles before the test drive (special-finance workflow). — [Kimoby](https://www.kimoby.com/blog/crm-car-dealership)

**After-sales / retention (equity mining)**
- Equity-mining appointment rates on contacted equity customers (AutoAlert, vendor): average 12–18%, strong 20–23%, top programs about 25%+. — [Spyne citing AutoAlert](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/dealership-ai-benchmarks)
- Dealership NPS fell from +48 to +29 in one year. Dealer AI adoption rose from 28% to 39% (CDK 2026 Friction Points Study). — [CDK Global (via ventavid compilation)](https://www.cdkglobal.com/insights/cdk-releases-2026-friction-points-study)

### Inferences
- The highest-value processes to replicate are: mandatory logging of every phone, walk-in and chat inquiry into the CRM (the Foureyes "never logged" figure grows every year); automatic after-hours first response; escalation rules when a lead is not touched within SLA; and explicit monitoring of AI-to-human handoff.
- Appointment set rate (around 40%) × show rate (around 59%) × show-to-sale is the standard funnel, and should be the backbone of any salesperson or BDC dashboard.
- Moving the credit pre-check and document collection online (in chat) addresses the largest online-step gap Cox measured, and suits the Thai finance-heavy used-car sale.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative benchmark comparing round-robin, shift/"up" rotation and ownership rules. Vendor articles describe these practices but publish no data.
- I found no independent data on test-drive-to-sale or trade-in capture rates for used cars.
- The BDC staffing ratios and calls-per-agent figures I searched for were not found in reliable sources.

## Q3. What does the data say about speed-to-lead, follow-up attempts, contact/show rates and lead-to-sale conversion?

### Takeaway
The evidence consistently shows that minutes matter. The classic HBR (2011) and InsideSales/MIT (2007) studies found 7x and 21x qualification lifts for one-hour and five-minute contact. Most buyers who buy do so within 3 days of first inquiry, and about 93% of eventually converted leads are reached by the 6th call. US dealers have improved (2026: 51% give a "perfect" multichannel response within 15 minutes; 61% respond within 15 minutes), but a large tail still takes hours, never responds, or responds without price, photos or alternatives.

### Cited Findings
**Classic cross-industry studies (dated)**
- Firms that contacted leads within an hour were nearly 7x as likely to qualify them as those contacting an hour later, and over 60x as likely as those waiting 24+ hours. Of 2,241 US companies audited, 37% responded within an hour, 24% took more than 24 hours and 23% never responded. The average response among responders was 42 hours. Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, HBR, March 2011 (1.25M leads). — [HBR](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)
- Odds of qualifying a lead were 21x higher when contacted within 5 minutes than at 30 minutes. Contact odds fell more than 10x within the first hour. Lead Response Management Study (InsideSales.com with Prof. James Oldroyd), 2007, 15,000+ leads and 100,000+ call attempts. — [LeadResponseManagement.org](https://www.leadresponsemanagement.org/lrm_study)
- Calling within the first minute raised conversion likelihood by 391%. 50% of leads are never called a second time. 93% of converted leads are reached by the 6th call attempt. Velocify "Ultimate Contact Strategy," about 3.5M leads, 400+ companies, 2012 data. — [Velocify PDF (via Salesforce AppExchange)](https://appexchange.salesforce.com/partners/servlet/servlet.FileDownload?file=00P3000000P3dgaEAB)

**Auto-specific response studies**
- DAS Technology (NADA 2025; 1,700+ dealers that were not DAS clients; leads Q3–Q4 2024): 61% responded within 15 minutes (55% in 2022), 19% took over an hour, 4% never responded, and 26% didn't include information on the vehicle asked about. 91% of replies excluded payment details, 90% omitted multiple photos, 74% gave no price quote, and 89% did not mention alternative in-stock vehicles. — [Digital Air Strike release](https://digitalairstrike.com/das-technology-unveils-comprehensive-automotive-dealership-lead-response-study-at-nada-2025/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250124160832/en/DAS-Technology-Unveils-Comprehensive-Automotive-Dealership-Lead-Response-Study-at-NADA-2025)
- Pied Piper Internet Lead Effectiveness (ILE) 2026 (3,290 dealerships, 33 brands; published Feb 23, 2026): industry average 71/100, up from 65 (fourth straight record). 51% gave a "perfect response" (phone plus email/text within 15 minutes), double the rate of five years earlier. Text replies were used 54% of the time (38% in 2025), phone 75% (66%), and phone plus email/text together 62% (49%). — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-finds-ai-raises-dealerships-internet-lead-responsiveness-but-creates-added-risks)
- Pied Piper dealer-group study (May 2025; 2,105 dealerships across 26 groups): only 60% answered a web lead by email or text within 1 hour. 40% scored above 80 and 19% below 40. — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/napleton-sets-record-in-topping-dealer-group-rankings-for-internet-lead-response)
- DAS mystery shop (Jan 2018; 1,500+ US dealers; web and Facebook Messenger leads): 16% responded within 15 minutes, 18% never responded, and the average response time exceeded 24 hours. Shoppers answered within 10 minutes were 3x more likely to visit. — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-dealership-online-lead-response-time-often-falls-short/)
- Clearline mystery shop of 53 Canadian dealers (April 2026): average first response 9.01 hours, median 11.5 hours, 28% never replied. Small vendor sample. — [Clearline](https://www.useclearline.com/blog/mystery-shop-dealership-lead-response-study-2026)
- Conversica: nearly 1 in 5 auto dealers did not respond to a direct inquiry (2016 data, published 2017). — [CBT News](https://www.cbtnews.com/conversica-sales-effectiveness-report/)

**Follow-up and conversion windows**
- 62.8% of sales leads did not hear from a salesperson within 24 hours of returning to the dealer site (about 65% for five prior years). 61.2% of leads who bought did so within 3 days of first inquiry (60% in 2024). 11.7% of all sales leads bought (10.2% prior year). Average hold time was about 73 seconds. — [Foureyes 2026](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/2026-automotive-dealer-benchmarks-report)

**Contact, appointment, show, close**
- Appointment set rates: internet leads 40–42%, phone leads 75–80% (Q4 2024–Apr 2025). Used-inventory internet leads set at 44–45% vs new at 35–38%. Used phone leads 77–81% vs new 71–79%. — [Foureyes (May 6, 2025)](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/dealership-appointment-set-rates)
- Q1 2026: contact 69%, set 40%, show 59%, close 18.5% (via Spyne). — [Spyne citing Foureyes](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/dealership-ai-benchmarks)
- Leads contacted within 5 minutes close at about 32% vs 12% for those contacted after 24 hours. This is an unverified vendor-blog claim with no primary study identified. — [search summary (Spyne/Proactive)](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/dealership-ai-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- A defensible SLA set for a used-car lot: first touch within 5 minutes (auto-reply plus a human within 15 minutes), including vehicle info, price or monthly payment, multiple photos and in-stock alternatives (the content failures DAS measured). Persist with at least 6 attempts across channels, because most conversions happen within 3 days and 93% of eventual converts are reached by attempt 6.
- Used-car leads set appointments at a higher rate than new-car leads (44% vs 35% internet), so the used-car benchmark for "good" should sit above the blended 40%.
- Phone-first follow-up roughly doubles set rate vs internet-only (75% vs 40%). In Thailand, the analogous comparison is a voice call vs chat-only on LINE/Facebook, which is untested in published data.

### Gaps
- No published lead-to-sale conversion specific to used-car dealers in Asia/Thailand.
- The Cox 2025/2026 total shopping time for used buyers was not obtained (the topline PDF could not be parsed).
- The "Phyllis-type" mystery-shop and DealerRater-specific lead-handling studies were not located. Pied Piper and DAS are the main mystery-shop sources found.

## Q4. How do dealers handle messaging channels (SMS, WhatsApp, Messenger, LINE) inside CRM, and how do they measure salesperson performance?

### Takeaway
In the US, SMS has become a core lead channel (54% of dealer replies used text in 2026) and modern CRMs such as DriveCentric unify text, email, call, video and chat into one feed. In Europe and India, WhatsApp is reached via connectors or AI agents (Keyloop AIME, LeadSquared plus Sinch/Spur). In Thailand, LINE OA is the main channel, but it lives in separate chat-inbox tools (Oho Chat, Readyplanet R-Chat, LINE OA chat tags) rather than in dealer CRMs. Performance is measured through response time, contact, set, show and close rates, plus logging compliance.

### Cited Findings
- DriveCentric's differentiator is a single conversation feed combining text, email, call, video and chat. — [Kimoby](https://www.kimoby.com/blog/crm-car-dealership)
- Pied Piper 2026: dealers answered by text 54% of the time (up from 38%) and by phone 75%. 62% combined phone with email/text. — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-finds-ai-raises-dealerships-internet-lead-responsiveness-but-creates-added-risks)
- 69% of US consumers would prefer that an unfamiliar company text rather than call them (Avochato survey, Dec 2019). 60% of dealer service customers prefer text during a visit (DriveSure, 2020). — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/study-shows-us-customers-prefer-texting-with-businesses-300974034.html); [DriveSure](https://drivesure.com/texting-is-now-the-top-preference-for-service-customers/)
- Keyloop/RAPID RTC handles live chat, social, text and video calls in the showroom. The planned AIME agent covers social, WhatsApp, SMS and email leads. — [newswire.ca](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/keyloop-acquires-rapid-rtc-and-enquirymax-885863074.html)
- LeadSquared plus Sinch WhatsApp API enables WhatsApp notifications, live-chat support and broadcasts. - LINE OA chat package features: chat tags, notes, longer chat history. LINE "MyCustomer" auto-adds friends and builds a customer database. — [LINE for Business TH](https://lineforbusiness.com/th/service/line-oa-features); [MyCustomer](https://lineforbusiness.com/th/service/mycustomer)

- Foureyes-type tools audit call recordings and website sessions to detect leads that salespeople never logged, which grade salesperson compliance independently of what reps enter. — [Foureyes 2025](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/2025-automotive-dealer-benchmarks-report)

### Inferences
- A Thai in-house CRM that ingests LINE OA and Facebook Messenger conversations directly onto the lead record (rather than leaving them in a separate inbox) would match the "single conversation feed" approach that is the leading feature among US CRMs.
- Pied Piper's warning applies directly: a dashboard can show "responded" while the customer experience failed. Periodic mystery shopping of one's own LINE OA and phone lines is the external check.
- Salesperson scorecards should combine speed (time to first human touch), activity (attempts within 3 days), funnel ratios (set, show, close) and hygiene (% of inquiries logged, % with next task scheduled).

### Gaps
- No published data on LINE-specific response times or conversion for Thai car dealers.
- No vendor documentation found for native LINE OA integration in any dealer-specific CRM.
- Commission and scorecard practices at Thai tents are undocumented.

## Q5. Used-car specifics: want lists, price-drop alerts, buying cars from customers, trade-in valuation

### Takeaway
Dealer CRMs support "want/wish lists" that alert staff when matching stock arrives (VinSolutions has had this for years). Newer tools match trade-ins and auction buys to stored buyer preferences automatically. On the acquisition side, a separate tool category (vAuto, DealerSocket Inventory+, CarGurus instant offers, VETTX, LeadLocate, VAN) sources cars from auctions, trade-ins and private sellers. I found no vendor documenting an end-to-end loop where a customer's want list automatically triggers sourcing.

### Cited Findings
- VinSolutions "Wish List": make/model/year/price on the customer record, with notifications to the salesperson or manager on a match (forum thread, about 15 years old). — [DealerRefresh](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/need-a-few-specific-crm-functions.1543/)
- Spyne: when a shopper's budget, model and mileage preferences are in the CRM, a new trade-in or auction pickup can be auto-matched to that buyer (vendor). — [Spyne](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/used-car-dealer-crm-software)
- The Bigin (Zoho) used-car template tracks inventory and matches cars to prospective buyers. Get My Auto treats past-customer databases as retargetable when inventory matches a known preference. — [Bigin](https://www.bigin.com/templates/used-car-sales.html); [Get My Auto](https://dealers.getmyauto.com/blog/get-my-auto-the-auto-dealer-crm-built-for-used-and-independent-dealerships)
- Cars Commerce markets "Shopper Alerts" that flag in the CRM when a dealer's lead is shopping competitors (title-level only; page not fetched). — [Cars Commerce](https://www.carscommerce.inc/shopper-alerts-competing-leads/)
- Acquisition tools:
  - vAuto Global Search unifies acquisition sources (auctions, trade-ins).
  - DealerSocket Inventory+ provides a buy/sell list.
  - CarGurus Instant Max Cash Offer lets dealers make instant offers on consumer cars.
  - VETTX aggregates Craigslist and Facebook Marketplace listings and automates seller outreach.
  - LeadLocate connects dealers with owners who requested an offer.
  - Vehicle Acquisition Network finds private sellers that match dealer criteria.
  
  — [vAuto](https://www.vauto.com/); [DealerSocket](https://dealersocket.com/products/inventory-management/); [VETTX](https://www.vettx.com/resources/blog/post/top-vehicle-sourcing-tools-dealers-are-using-in-2025); [LeadLocate](https://leadlocate.com/sales-leads/vehicle-acquisition-software); [VAN](https://www.buywithvan.com/); [Carketa list](https://carketa.com/the-top-10-tools-for-car-dealers-to-acquire-new-inventory/)
- DAS 2025: 89% of dealer replies didn't mention alternative in-stock vehicles. This is a missed matching opportunity when the asked-for car is sold. — [Digital Air Strike](https://digitalairstrike.com/das-technology-unveils-comprehensive-automotive-dealership-lead-response-study-at-nada-2025/)
- 42% of customers inquiring about a new vehicle ended up buying used (DAS 2018). — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-dealership-online-lead-response-time-often-falls-short/)
- Equity mining (contacting existing customers whose car is worth more than the loan payoff) yields 12–18% appointment rates on average (AutoAlert, vendor). For a used lot, this doubles as a buy-back/acquisition channel. — [Spyne citing AutoAlert](https://www.spyne.ai/blogs/dealership-ai-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- For a Thai used-car dealer, the high-leverage features are:
  - a structured "car wanted" record (brand, model, year range, budget or down payment/monthly installment, gear, color) with auto-match when stock is added;
  - auto-suggesting 2–3 in-stock alternatives in the first reply when the asked-for car is sold;
  - a separate "customer wants to sell" lead type with its own appraisal workflow (photos, mileage, finance payoff balance, offer, follow-up);
  - reverse matching, where an incoming seller's car is checked against open want-lists before pricing the offer.
- Thai finance-led buying (down payment/monthly) means the want-list should store payment capacity and credit pre-check status, not just vehicle attributes (consistent with ProMax's credit-first approach in Q1).

### Gaps
- Price-drop alerts: I found no dealer-CRM documentation of automatic price-drop notifications to past inquirers. The feature exists on consumer marketplaces but was not sourced in this session.
- No independent data on want-list hit rates or the conversion lift of automated matching.
- I found no Thai trade-in valuation tool comparable to KBB or vAuto. Thai valuation appears to rely on auction results (e.g., Toyota's Auction Express; see Q6) and dealer judgment, which is unverified.

## Q6. Thailand/SEA: how do Thai used-car dealers and platforms manage leads; are there Thai dealer CRM products or published practices?

### Takeaway
I found no Thai dealer-specific CRM with published lead-handling practices. Thai tents typically run a local stock/accounting program (e.g., COMMACAR/TENTCAR, BossCarSoft) plus LINE OA and Facebook as the de facto lead channel, often handled in a chat inbox (Oho Chat, Readyplanet). Platforms (one2car, Carsome, Carro, Toyota Sure, Kaidee) generate leads or supply inventory but publish little about dealer lead management. The market is still mostly "unorganized" (about 68% of 2025 trades per one market report), and trust is the main barrier the platforms emphasize.

### Cited Findings
**Thai dealer software**
- COMMACAR (marketed as "TENTCAR", by Twin Software Solutions): used-car lot system with vehicle records, reconditioning jobs, customers and referrers, income and expenses, payroll, calendar, dashboard, and an auto-generated public storefront from stock. — [software-listing.com](https://software-listing.com/q/car-dealer-software-thailand)
- BossCarSoft: modular dealership system (showroom, hire-purchase, parts, registration, insurance) with customer history per transaction. The same source treats chat and lead capture as a separate layer from dealer systems. — [software-listing.com](https://software-listing.com/q/car-dealer-software-thailand)

**Market context**
- Thai used-car sales were about THB 140B in 2021 (+7.5%, Kasikorn Research), with about 1.2M units. The Association of Used Cars partnered with Japan's Goo for inspections to build trust. Dealers were being forced to operate both online and offline. 21% of Thai consumers prefer fully or partly virtual transactions (Deloitte 2022). — [KrASIA/Nikkei (Mar 2022)](https://amp.kr-asia.com/thai-used-car-market-heats-up-as-foreign-startups-jump-in)
- Unorganized vendors held 67.71% of 2025 trades. ### Inferences
- A Thai dealer's in-house CRM that unifies LINE OA and Facebook chats, lead records, want-lists, stock matching and finance pre-check would already exceed the typical Thai tent stack (stock program plus chat inbox) and resemble the US "single conversation feed" CRMs functionally.
- Trust is the dominant theme for Thai platforms (inspection, certification, warranties). A CRM's first reply should therefore send proof assets (inspection reports, multiple photos, clear price and monthly installment). This aligns with DAS's US finding that most replies lack price, photos and payment details.
- Because Thai platforms (one2car, Carsome auctions, Toyota Sure/Auction Express) are inventory sources as much as lead sources, a used-car CRM should tag lead source by platform to measure cost per sale per channel. The US Foureyes analysis did this and found forms and paid channels convert best.

### Gaps
- No published speed-to-lead, contact-rate or conversion benchmarks for Thai or SEA car dealers.
- No Thai-language case study of a tent using a CRM end to end. Carsome, Carro and Kaidee B2B dealer tools (dealer apps, lead dashboards) were not documented in sources reached. Kaidee's car-dealer lead products were not covered.
- Carsome Thailand dealer count for 2025, Carro Thailand's current status, and Cars24 Thailand's current status (launched Nov 2021 per KrASIA; current presence not verified) are all unknown.
