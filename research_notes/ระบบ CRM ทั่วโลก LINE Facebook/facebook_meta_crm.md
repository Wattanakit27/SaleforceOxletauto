# CRM on Facebook/Meta Messaging (Messenger, Instagram Direct, WhatsApp Business Platform, Lead Ads), state as of Oct 2026

Research date: 2026-10-12. Scope: what Meta offers natively, what the Meta APIs allow a third-party or in-house CRM to do, and which Meta-centric chat/CRM tools exist. The comparison target is Oxlet Auto's in-house CRM, which ingests Facebook Page Messenger chats. Its current state is in the sibling note `oxlet_current_system.md`: FB arrives by Conversations API polling every 2 minutes while the webhook waits for App Review and Business Verification, and staff replies from Business Suite come back as echoes. Meta developer docs (developers.facebook.com, new `/documentation/business-messaging/...` tree) are treated as primary. Vendor blogs and third-party pricing aggregators are flagged as such. Conflicts are flagged inline. LINE specifics and general multi-channel CRMs are out of scope here (other researchers).

---

## Q1. What CRM tooling does Meta offer natively in 2025–2026 (Business Suite inbox, Leads Center, Instant Forms, Click-to-Messenger/WhatsApp ads, Marketing Messages, Business AI / Business Agent, Meta Verified / Meta One)?

### Takeaway
สรุป (TH): Meta มี "CRM เบา ๆ" ให้ฟรีใน Business Suite. ได้แก่ inbox รวม FB/IG/WhatsApp ที่มี label, note, assign และ auto-reply พื้นฐาน กับ Leads Center ที่เป็น pipeline แบบ Kanban (Intake → Qualified → Converted, ปรับเองได้). แต่ **ไม่มี API หรือ webhook สำหรับ stage ของ Leads Center** และเก็บข้อมูล instant form แค่ 90 วัน. ในปี 2025–2026 Meta เน้นสองเรื่อง (1) ข้อความแบบเสียเงินที่ทักลูกค้านอกหน้าต่าง 24 ชม. ได้ คือ Marketing Messages ซึ่งไทยใช้ได้ และมาแทน Recurring Notifications ที่ปิดไปเมื่อ 10 ก.พ. 2026 (2) AI agent: "Business AI" บน Messenger ไทยเปิดทดลองฟรี มี.ค. 2026 และ "Meta Business Agent" เปิดทั่วโลก 3 มิ.ย. 2026 ที่ qualify lead, นัดหมาย และส่งต่อให้คนได้. ส่วน Meta One (15 ก.ย. 2026) เป็น subscription ที่รวม verified badge กับโควตา Business Agent.

EN: Meta's native stack (free Business Suite inbox + Leads Center) covers labels, notes, assignment, simple automations and a lightweight lead pipeline. It is a closed UI: Leads Center stages have no public API or webhook. 2025–26 investment went into paid out-of-window messaging (Marketing Messages, replacing Recurring Notifications) and AI agents (Business AI → Meta Business Agent), monetized via Meta One.

### Cited Findings

**Business Suite / Page inbox (shared inbox for FB, IG, WhatsApp)**
- Business Suite shows Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Business app conversations in one list, with platform filters and quick replies — [martechvibe](https://martechvibe.com/?p=18281) (secondary)
- Native inbox automations (third-party descriptions; I did not reach Meta's own help page): Instant Reply, Away message, FAQ replies, Location, Contact information, Hours, Custom keywords, Comment-to-message. The inbox also identifies unanswered messages and assigns conversations, and it is free — [instantdm 2026](https://instantdm.com/blog/best-facebook-auto-reply-tools-2026); [statusbrew](https://statusbrew.com/insights/setting-up-facebook-auto-replies); [sleekflow](https://sleekflow.io/en-us/blog/facebook-messenger-auto-reply)
- Vendor-reported limits: the Custom keywords template is capped at 5 keywords; Custom keywords and Comment-to-message are desktop-only; native auto-replies are around 500 characters; an "away" status can suppress instant replies — [instantdm](https://instantdm.com/blog/best-facebook-auto-reply-tools-2026); [spurnow](https://www.spurnow.com/blogs/facebook-messenger-auto-reply) (vendor claims, unverified against Meta)
- Shared-inbox features: labels, notes, customer details, assignment and follow-up management. Vendors say it hits a ceiling at round-robin routing, CRM sync and multi-step flows — [statusbrew](https://statusbrew.com/insights/setting-up-facebook-auto-replies); [instantdm](https://instantdm.com/blog/best-facebook-auto-reply-tools-2026)
- Inbox labels are exposed via API. The Custom Labels API creates, updates and deletes labels "for its messaging experience and its Facebook Page Inbox": POST `/PAGE-ID/custom_labels`, attach with POST `/<LABEL_ID>/label` with `user`=PSID, read with GET `/<PSID>/custom_labels`. The `inbox_labels` webhook (v12.0+) fires when a label is assigned to or removed from a person's thread. It requires `pages_show_list`, `pages_manage_metadata` and `pages_me`, a token from someone with the MESSAGING task, and acceptance of the Page Contact Terms. Lead-gen ad threads get predefined labels such as "Lead Complete" and "Lead Incomplete". Label lookups are not supported for accounts created with a phone number — [Meta Custom Labels](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/identity/custom-labels)

**Leads Center (Business Suite) — "Meta's built-in CRM"**
- Default pipeline: **Intake → Qualified → Converted**, shown as Kanban plus a sortable table. Stages can be renamed or added (e.g., "Contacted", "Quoted") and leads can be marked lost. New leads land in Intake and stay there until moved manually (article published 10 Jul 2026, updated 1 Sep 2026) — [LeadSync blog](https://leadsync.me/blog/managing-leads-using-the-facebook-lead-ads-center/) (vendor of a sync tool)
- Lead sources: FB/IG lead ads (Instant Forms), **Messenger and Instagram conversations**, and manual or CSV import. Features: filters (campaign, form, date, stage, source, owner, label), assignment to Business Suite users, notes, labels, scheduled reminders, replying to Messenger/IG leads from the dashboard, CSV export/import — [LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/managing-leads-using-the-facebook-lead-ads-center/)
- Limitations: no automated follow-up, no email/SMS sending, no instant per-lead notifications, not in the Business Suite mobile app, basic reporting, and **Meta keeps Instant Form lead data for 90 days** — [LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/managing-leads-using-the-facebook-lead-ads-center/). Desktop-only is also stated by [Metricool](https://metricool.com/de/meta-business-suite-tutorial/)
- Stage names conflict across sources: Intake/Qualified/Converted ([LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/managing-leads-using-the-facebook-lead-ads-center/)); New/Contacted/Qualified/Unqualified/Converted/Not Interested ([Dental Marketing SOP](https://grow.dentalmarketing.com/knowledge/how-to-access-and-manage-leads-in-metas-lead-center)); Raw/In-progress/Won/Archived/Bad ([OOM](https://www.oom.com.sg/how-to-master-meta-business-suite-a-complete-guide/)). The UI has likely changed over time; none of these cite Meta.
- Export requires the "Leads access" permission granted by a Page admin. CSV download by date range for import into a CRM — [Dental Marketing](https://grow.dentalmarketing.com/knowledge/how-to-access-and-manage-leads-in-metas-lead-center)
- **No API or webhook for Leads Center stage.** A developer (20 Jul 2026) found no Graph API endpoint for the current pipeline stage and no webhook on stage change. The UI uses an internal GraphQL mutation (`LeadOpportunityStageUpdateMutation`) that third parties can't use. The suggested workaround: staff apply an **inbox label** (e.g., "send to CRM") and catch the label webhook. No accepted solution — [Make community](https://community.make.com/t/how-can-i-detect-leads-center-pipeline-stage-in-make/112217)

**Instant Forms (Lead Ads) and Messenger lead-gen ads**
- Click-to-Messenger ads with the **"Generate Leads" automated chat template** (Messages objective → Optimization: Leads) qualify leads in a Q&A flow. It supports pre-filled or free-text answers, disqualifying questions and reminders if the user doesn't finish. Field IDs can be renamed to match a CRM. Afterwards the Page continues manually in the Page inbox or via a third-party live-chat app — [Meta Messaging Ads doc](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads); [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/facebook-messenger-ads-to-generate-leads/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/facebook-rolls-out-automated-lead-generation-in-messenger/)
- API mechanics: the **Messenger Leadgen app (App ID 413038776280800)** takes thread control and asks the ad's questions. At the end, control passes to your app if one is defined, otherwise it is released. Your app's control lasts 1 day from the last user message by default, extendable to 7 days via `receiving_app_control_expiration` (1–7). "The Page Inbox can always be used to control the thread and reply." — [Meta Messenger Lead Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/conversation-routing/messenger-lead-ads)
- How the answers reach an app: (1) a summary-message webhook, only if summary messages are enabled and the lead completes; (2) the `messaging_handovers` webhook, whose metadata is `messenger_lead_gen_complete` for completed leads and a different value for incomplete or disqualified ones; (3) the Conversations API for the full Q&A thread. Webhooks are not sent to apps while the ad flow runs. Prerequisites: `pages_messaging` and `pages_manage_metadata`, subscriptions to `messages` and `messaging_handovers`, the app authorized in the Page's Advanced Messaging settings, and Conversation Routing enabled — [Meta Messenger Lead Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/conversation-routing/messenger-lead-ads)
- Custom thread control windows for Click-to-Messenger ads became available on 22 Jul 2025. Welcome Message flow ads put a `flow_id` in `messaging_referrals` (24 Feb 2025) — [Messenger changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/changelog)

**Click-to-Message ads (Messenger/IG/WhatsApp) — performance claims in Thailand**
- Meta Business Messaging Summit, Bangkok (article 24 Jul 2025): across a review of 28 A/B tests, CTM ads had ">99% probability" of outperforming and a "median 20% lower cost per purchase". Opportunity Score early adopters saw a median 12% lower cost per result. **"Transfer with Your Payment App" is fully available in Thailand**, so users pay in Messenger via K PLUS, Krungthai NEXT or SCB EASY — [The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/24/07/2025/158757) (Meta claims, global)
- Kantar 2025 study cited by Meta for Thailand: 81% of Thai adults message a business at least weekly; 73% discovered new small or local businesses via messaging; 74% prefer talking to brands like friends; 68% find AI chatbots helpful; 95% of surveyed Thai businesses report better CX; "55% increase in converting leads into sales". Meta also cites "600 million conversations ... every day" between people and businesses — [The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/24/07/2025/158757). The figure that 80% of Thais say messaging is their main channel with businesses comes from [Brand Buffet, Mar 2026](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/03/meta-launchs-business-ai-via-messenger-for-thai-sme/)

**Marketing Messages on Messenger (paid, out-of-window promos) — replaces Recurring Notifications**
- Launched to all tech providers on **1 Jul 2025**. It is paid per message *delivered*, billed on the Meta ads invoice, with a daily or lifetime budget; bid optional; an Estimation API is available. Messages can be sent outside the 24h window and stay in the thread — [Meta Marketing Messages FAQ](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/marketing-messages-on-messenger/faq)
- **Thailand is an eligible market**, along with AU, BR, CL, CO, HK, IN, ID, IL, MY, MX, NZ, PE, PH, SA, SG, TW, AE, US and VN. Tech providers can only serve businesses there — [Meta FAQ](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/marketing-messages-on-messenger/faq)
- Opt-in routes: users who click and respond to Click-to-Messenger ads; **uploading a CRM list of phone numbers and/or emails (matched profiles are subscribed automatically)**; an opt-in template sent in chat. **PSIDs cannot be uploaded.** Every message carries an opt-out link. Frequency cap: 1 marketing message per user per day per Page — [Meta FAQ](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/marketing-messages-on-messenger/faq)
- Ads Manager availability is contradictory: one FAQ section says "no launch date to share", while other sections describe an Ads Manager send option — [Meta FAQ](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/marketing-messages-on-messenger/faq). A Jon Loomer post is titled "New Feature: Marketing Messages from Meta Business Suite" ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/marketing-messages-from-meta-business-suite/)), but I did not read its content.
- **Recurring Notifications (RN) deprecated.** No new integrations from 1 Sep 2025. Existing sends were cut from 1 per 24h to 1 per 48h. Discontinued **10 Feb 2026** globally except AU, EU, JP, KR and UK, where the API now returns errors. The same FAQ also states 12 Jan 2026 (internal conflict) — [Meta FAQ](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/marketing-messages-on-messenger/faq). 10 Feb is also reported by [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/metas-recurring-marketing-messages-api-will-end-this-week/811668/); Omnichat said 7 Jan 2026 ([Omnichat](https://blog.omnichat.ai/facebook-recurring-notification-message/amp/))
- Some ManyChat users reported losing RN before getting Marketing Messages access, leaving no way to message outside 24h (anecdotal) — [ManyChat community](https://community.manychat.com/product-updates/marketing-messages-on-messenger-8203)

**Business AI → Meta Business Agent**
- **Business AI on Messenger launched in Thailand as a free trial (article 11 Mar 2026).** It works with Facebook Pages only (not IG yet). The Philippines came first, then Thailand, with Vietnam "coming soon"; it was not launched on Messenger in the US. It replies 24/7 as a "sales assistant", learns from Page posts, ads, catalog and website, and its tone is configurable. Meta named **car dealers** among the sectors that use chat most for sales — [Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/03/meta-launchs-business-ai-via-messenger-for-thai-sme/); also [The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/?p=182027)
- **Meta Business Agent**, globally available on **3 Jun 2026** (Conversations conference, London), runs on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. It answers business-specific questions, recommends from the catalogue, books appointments, **qualifies leads**, completes sales, replies in the customer's language, and lets the business define when a human steps in. It ended about 2 years of limited testing; Meta says more than 1M businesses had used a version on WhatsApp and Messenger. Free initially, paid plans "in the coming months" — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/meta-unveils-ai-business-agent-platform-for-whatsapp-instagram-and-messenger-534739-2026-06-03); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-business-agent-brings-ai-customer-service-to-whatsapp-globally/)
- It also summarizes overnight chats. The enterprise **Business Agent Platform** integrates with Shopify, Zendesk and Shopee, with "enterprise-grade controls, guardrails and measurement". Enterprises are expected to pay by token usage. The earlier free test was named "Business AI" (late 2025, e.g., Mexico, India) — [iStart, 10 Jun 2026](https://istart.com.au/?p=43890)
- Conflicting report: CX Today (8 Jul 2026) places the launch at a Mumbai Business Summit — [CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/?p=991187). The London 3 Jun date is better supported.

### Inferences
- Leads Center could stand in for a CRM at a small dealer, but it can't feed an in-house CRM. There is no stage API, the 90-day lead retention is short, and there is no mobile access. For Oxlet, the in-house CRM should stay the system of record. The documented bridge between Meta's UI and the in-house CRM is **inbox labels**: `inbox_labels` webhook in, Custom Labels API out. Leads Center stages are not a usable bridge.
- Marketing Messages is the only compliant way to proactively re-contact Messenger leads after 24h, outside HUMAN_AGENT (manual, 7 days, needs approval). Thailand is eligible, and opt-in can come from an uploaded CRM phone list. This matters for following up cold used-car leads, but it costs money per delivered message.
- Business AI / Business Agent overlaps with what an in-house CRM's auto-qualification would do. The free Thai Messenger trial is a low-cost experiment, but how it coexists with Oxlet's app under Conversation Routing (who "owns" the thread) is undocumented.

### Gaps
- I could not open Meta's own Business Help Center pages for inbox automations or Leads Center. Feature lists above come from vendor or third-party guides, and stage names conflict.
- No official Meta statement found on whether Leads Center stages for *messaging* leads (as opposed to Instant Forms) are retained beyond 90 days.
- No Thai baht price found for standalone Meta Verified for business. A consumer figure (429 THB/mo) appeared in a Thai news search snippet but could not be tied to a dated source. It is unclear whether Meta One Essential supersedes Meta Verified for Pages in Thailand.
- No details on Business Agent handoff mechanics (inbox folder, thread control, webhooks to other apps) or paid pricing in Thailand.

### Cited Findings

**Standard messaging window and message tags**
- "The Standard Messaging Window is the 24 hour time period in which you are allowed to send a message to a person." It opens when a person messages the Page, taps a CTA, reacts or comments, among other triggers. Promotional content is allowed inside the window. An expired window returns error **1545041** — [Meta Send Messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/send-messages)
- `messaging_type` values: Response, Updates (both in-window), and Tagged Message (out-of-window, non-promotional only). "Message Tags may not be used to send promotional content", and misuse can restrict the Page — [Meta Send Messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/send-messages)
- **HUMAN_AGENT tag**: a human agent may reply within **7 days** of the user's message, for issues that can't be resolved in 24h (e.g., weekend closures). It is **not available in Standard Access or development mode** and requires App Review ("App dashboard → App review → Permissions & Features → Human Agent") with a demonstration, plus **Business Verification** and possibly extra contracts. Automated messages are prohibited — [Meta Human Agent feature](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/features-reference/human-agent); [Chatwoot help](https://chatwoot.com/hc/user-guide/articles/1745225158-what-is-human-agent-tag-in-instagram-messenger-channel)
- **Deprecated tags**: requests with CONFIRMED_EVENT_UPDATE, ACCOUNT_UPDATE or POST_PURCHASE_UPDATE return **error code 100**. Migrate to **Utility Templates** or the **Marketing Messages API**. Date conflict: the changelog says **27 Mar 2026** ([Messenger changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/changelog)); the Send Messages page says "Effective April 27th, 2026" ([Meta Send Messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/send-messages))
- Recurring Notifications ended 10 Feb 2026 outside AU/EU/JP/KR/UK (see Q1) — [Meta FAQ](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/marketing-messages-on-messenger/faq). One-Time Notification is still recommended for out-of-window follow-ups by the Send Messages page — [Meta Send Messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/send-messages) (its current status was not otherwise confirmed)

**message_echoes (do staff replies from Business Suite reach a third-party system?)**
- "This callback occurs when your page sends a message." Payload: `is_echo` (true when sent by the Page), `app_id` (the sending app), `metadata` (only if set in the original Send API call) and `mid`. Supported types: text, image, video, audio, sticker, template, fallback, appointment booking, products and replies. You subscribe via the `message_echoes` webhook field. Echoes can arrive inside a `standby` array — [Meta message_echoes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/webhooks/webhook-events/message-echoes)
- "Starting with Graph API v12.0, the app_id field returns the **Facebook Page inbox app ID (26390203743090)** whenever the message is sent via Facebook Page inbox." This lets a CRM tell human-inbox replies from its own API sends — [Meta message_echoes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/webhooks/webhook-events/message-echoes)
- `message_echoes` is a separate field for Messenger only. On Instagram, echoes come inside the `messages` subscription — [Meta webhook reference (search snippet)](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/reference/webhook-events/message-echoes)
- Product echoes need `catalog_management` and v8.0+. Template attachment IDs in echoes are numbers, not strings — [Meta message_echoes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/webhooks/webhook-events/message-echoes)
- Oxlet's own system already observes that replies typed in the Facebook/Business Suite app come back as echoes and stop the SLA clock. Page auto-replies within 15 s are not counted — [oxlet_current_system.md (sibling note)](oxlet_current_system.md)
- Contrast given in the research brief: LINE does not deliver replies sent from LINE OA Manager to the webhook (LINE details covered by another researcher).

**Conversation Routing (replaces Handover Protocol)**
- Meta: it "no longer supports Handover Protocol for Messenger and all the businesses are migrated to Conversation Routing". It is backward compatible with most Handover Protocol APIs. Multiple connected apps share one inbox, and a designated app is responsible for responding. For Messenger, only **campaign routing** is available as an entry-point routing option; link and social routing are not — [Meta Conversation Routing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/conversation-routing)
- If a Page has a Default App for Conversation Routing and calls Handover APIs, it gets the error "Handover Protocol APIs disabled for this page". Pass and release thread control still work for now, but Meta says to move to the Send API — [Meta Handover Protocol (legacy page)](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/handover-protocol)
- Instagram Handover Protocol ended 23 Oct 2025. On 13 Oct 2025 an `is_owner` field was added to the Conversations API when Conversation Routing is enabled; it is returned only if requested. On 11 Jun 2024 app-level thread control configs replaced the deprecated default-app takeover config — [Messenger changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/changelog); [Flyweight](https://flyweight.io/articles/meta-conversation-routing-business-manager)
- Legacy semantics, still useful background: the Page Inbox is every Page's default human-agent app. In handover-era Pages, marking a thread "Done" in the inbox released control — [Meta Handover Protocol](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/handover-protocol); [Cognigy](https://support.cognigy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016021919-Facebook-Messenger-Use-Facebook-Handover)

**Conversations API (history / polling)**
- Endpoints: `GET /PAGE-ID/conversations?platform=messenger` (+`user_id=PSID` for one person), `GET /CONVERSATION-ID?fields=messages`, and `GET /MESSAGE-ID?fields=id,created_time,from,to,message,reply_to` — [Meta Conversations API](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/conversations)
- **"you can only get details about the 20 most recent messages in the conversation"**; older ones return a deleted-message error. Conversations in the **Requests folder inactive for 30 days are not returned**. Messenger permissions: `pages_manage_metadata`, `pages_read_engagement` and `pages_messaging`, with a token from someone holding the MESSAGING or MODERATE task. **"Advanced Access is required to access conversations"** with people who have no role — [Meta Conversations API](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/conversations)

### Inferences
- **Key differentiator vs LINE**: Meta echoes human replies sent from the Page inbox/Business Suite, distinguishable by `app_id` 26390203743090. So an in-house CRM can log every staff reply and measure response SLA without forcing staff into the CRM UI. Oxlet already relies on this. Business Suite inbox is the same "Page inbox" app; Meta's doc names "Facebook Page inbox" only, but Oxlet's observation confirms Business Suite replies echo.
- Polling via the Conversations API is viable at Oxlet's scale. At 2 calls/s/Page, a 2-minute cycle allows about 240 calls per Page. Meta documents a **20-most-recent-messages detail limit**, which would cap long backfills. However, Oxlet's own sync pages through messages newest→oldest and has stored ≥23,800 FB messages across ≥800 threads (per project notes), so in practice the limit seems to hit the per-`/MESSAGE-ID` detail call rather than field-expanded `messages{…}` paging. This is unverified, and Oxlet should test it on a long thread. The webhook path removes both concerns.
- The documented "no App Review for your own Page" statement conflicts with the Conversations API doc and with Oxlet's experience. Oxlet should test webhook delivery for non-role users in Standard/Limited Access before assuming review is mandatory. **HUMAN_AGENT (7-day replies from the CRM) definitely needs App Review + Business Verification**, so Business Verification is worth finishing anyway.
- After 27 Mar/27 Apr 2026 the only out-of-window options are HUMAN_AGENT (manual, 7 days), Utility templates (non-promotional), and paid Marketing Messages. RN and the three deprecated tags are gone.
- The CRM should download attachments to its own storage at ingest time; CDN URLs are signed. Handle `fallback` without a URL, and handle the 30 Aug 2026 sticker type change, including in echoes.
- If Oxlet ever runs Messenger lead-gen ads or Business AI alongside its app, Conversation Routing ownership (`is_owner`) and thread-control windows decide which app may reply. The Page Inbox can always take control.

## Q3. Conversions API for CRM / "Conversion Leads" optimization and Conversions API for Business Messaging: how dealers send CRM stages back to Meta

### Takeaway
สรุป (TH): มีสองท่อแยกกัน (1) **Conversions API for CRM (Conversion Leads)** ใช้ได้กับ **Instant Forms เท่านั้น**: ส่ง stage ใน CRM (lead → qualified → sold) พร้อม `lead_id` 15–17 หลัก โดยต้องมี ≥200 lead/เดือน, stage เป้าหมายต้องเกิดภายใน 28 วัน และมี conversion rate 1–40% (2) **Conversions API for Business Messaging** สำหรับแชทจาก Click-to-Messenger ส่ง event เช่น `LeadSubmitted`, `QualifiedLead`, `Purchase` โดยผูกด้วย `page_id` + `page_scoped_user_id` (PSID) เพื่อให้ Meta optimize โฆษณาทักแชทหา "คนที่ซื้อจริง" ต้องได้สิทธิ์ `page_events` ระดับ Advanced/Full Access ทางนี้เหมาะกับ Oxlet ที่สุด เพราะลีดส่วนใหญ่เข้ามาทางแชท ไม่ใช่ฟอร์ม

### Cited Findings

**Conversions API for CRM → Conversion Leads performance goal (Instant Forms)**
- "The Conversion Leads performance goal is currently only compatible with Facebook/Instagram's Lead Ads (Instant Forms)." — [Meta Conversion Leads Integration](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration)
- Eligibility: "Generate at least 200 leads per month"; "Can upload data regularly at least once per day"; the optimized stage "occurs within 28 days of leads being generated"; it "has a conversion rate between 1% - 40%" — [Meta Conversion Leads Integration](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration)
- Data: store the **15–17 digit Meta Lead ID** in the CRM ("Sending lead IDs for each event is recommended"). Otherwise send click ID, phone or email — [Meta Conversion Leads Integration](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration). The partner guide calls `lead_id` mandatory — [Meta CAPI for CRM: Integrate as a Platform](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/guides/conversions-api-for-crm) (soft conflict)
- Payload: `event_name` is free-form (your CRM stage names). Send **every** stage as it updates, including the initial lead, and all previous stages before "Converted". `event_time` is when the CRM stage changed, up to 7 days before sending and after lead creation. `action_source` = `system_generated`; `custom_data.event_source` = `crm`; `lead_event_source` = the CRM name — [Meta Payload Specification](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration/payload-specification)
- Timeline: connect the CRM to Lead Ads (prerequisite) → pixel/dataset (<1 day) → CAPI connection (<1 day via partner, **3–4 weeks custom**) → Meta data verification (1–2 days) → funnel config → learning phase (2–4 weeks). Total time to value is about 1 month — [Meta Conversion Leads Integration](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration). Partners: build 3–7 weeks; onboarding an advertiser to fully optimized campaigns 1.5–2 months — [Meta CAPI for CRM partner guide](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/guides/conversions-api-for-crm)
- No-code paths: Salesforce Webhooks (lead status changes auto-send events) — [Meta Salesforce Webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/guides/salesforce-webhooks); Zapier integration guide — [Meta Zapier step 1](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration/zapier/step-1-implement-integration)

**Conversions API for Business Messaging (Click-to-Messenger / WhatsApp / IG chats)**
- Purpose: Business Messaging partners or advertisers send events about customer interactions in chats started from ads that click to WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram — [Meta CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
- Messenger event shape: `action_source: "business_messaging"`, `messaging_channel: "messenger"`, `user_data: {page_id, page_scoped_user_id}`. WhatsApp uses `whatsapp_business_account_id` + `ctwa_clid` (required; Biz API 2.45.1+). IG uses `instagram_business_account_id` + `ig_sid` — [Meta CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
- Standard events: Purchase, **LeadSubmitted**, InitiateCheckout, AddToCart, ViewContent, OrderCreated/Shipped/Delivered/Canceled/Returned, CartAbandoned, **QualifiedLead**, RatingProvided, ReviewProvided — [Meta CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
- Permissions: Messenger needs **`page_events` (advanced/full access)** + Marketing API Access Tier; WhatsApp needs `whatsapp_business_management` + `whatsapp_business_manage_events`; IG needs `instagram_manage_events`. Only one dataset can be linked per Page. Attribution follows the page/dataset ID, not the app ID. "Meta does not assist with deduplicating events". If conversion happens off-thread (e.g., in showroom), "you should still send that event back to Meta" — [Meta CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
- Optimization: the current doc says purchase optimization is supported for Messenger and WhatsApp but **not Instagram** (optimize IG for conversations instead) — [Meta CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging). An older FAQ snippet said purchase optimization was for Click-to-Messenger only, not WhatsApp — [Meta onboarding guide (search snippet)](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging/) (conflict; newer page wins)
- Full Access threshold "reduced from 1,500 to 500 Marketing API calls in the past 15 days", but the body text still says 1,500 (internal inconsistency) — [Meta CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
- The older "Messaging Events API" was slated for deprecation (Sep 2025) in favor of sending through the main Conversions API (search-snippet only; page returned 403) — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/messaging-events-api/)
- Chat-CRM vendors already ship this: Kommo sends Lead and Purchase signals for CTM leads — [Kommo KB](https://www.kommo.com/support/kb/capi-overview/); Blip and Netcore also offer "Lead"/"Purchase" signals — [Blip](https://help.blip.ai/hc/en-us/articles/18502037660695)

**Automotive practice**
- Driftrock (an automotive CAPI vendor) argues that dealers typically send only "form submitted", which trains Meta to find form-fillers. Its integrations send valid leads, qualified leads, booked test drives and closed sales. It calls DIY multi-platform CAPI (7+ APIs) costly and says Conversion Leads Optimization is hard to implement — [Driftrock](https://www.driftrock.com/features/conversion-apis) (vendor)

### Inferences
- For Oxlet, whose leads are mostly organic or CTM Messenger chats rather than Instant Forms, **Conversion Leads optimization does not apply** unless it runs Instant Form campaigns with ≥200 leads/month. The relevant pipe is **CAPI for Business Messaging**: send `LeadSubmitted` when a chat becomes a lead in the CRM (phone captured), `QualifiedLead` when sales qualifies it (e.g., finance pre-check or appointment), and `Purchase` (with value = car price) when sold. Key it on `page_id` + PSID, which the CRM already stores. Off-thread sales still count.
- Prerequisites stack up: `page_events` Full Access means App Review plus Marketing API access tier. This is another reason to complete Business Verification.
- Because PSIDs are per-Page, a buyer who chatted on both Oxlet Pages has two PSIDs. Events should be sent for the PSID and Page where the ad-driven chat happened.

### Gaps
- Not documented which attribution window CAPI for Business Messaging uses, or whether "QualifiedLead" can be an optimization goal for CTM ads (only purchase optimization is confirmed).
- No public case study found of a Thai or SEA used-car dealer sending CRM stages via CAPI.
- I couldn't confirm whether Leads Center "Converted" stage changes are fed to Meta automatically as conversion signals.

At 10–15 sales users, Kommo costs roughly $350–525/mo at Advanced pricing, and respond.io Growth is about $159–199/mo plus seats. Neither has a native LINE OA depth comparable to Thai tools; that comparison belongs to the LINE researcher.
- Every Meta-API-based tool faces the same platform limits as Oxlet: the 24h window, HUMAN_AGENT review, RN removal, and the 20-message Conversations API cap. Tools mainly differ in UX and automation, not data access.

### Gaps
- Official ManyChat, Chatfuel and Pancake price lists could not be read (403 or prices not rendered). Figures are third-party and conflicting.
- No Zoho CRM Messenger integration details were found.
- No Thai baht pricing for Zwiz tiers beyond "from 500 THB/month".
- No evidence found of a Thai used-car dealer using any of these tools publicly.

## Q5. Automotive use: Messenger, Marketplace, Automotive Inventory Ads (vehicle catalog), lead ads; data on response time and conversion via Messenger

### Takeaway
สรุป (TH): Meta มี **Automotive Inventory Ads (AIA)** ที่ใช้ vehicle catalog ยิงโฆษณารถรายคัน (ระดับ VIN). ตั้งแต่ **30 ม.ค. 2023 ดีลเลอร์ลงรถใน Marketplace ผ่านเพจธุรกิจไม่ได้แล้ว** ต้องใช้ AIA แบบเสียเงินแทน. หลักฐานที่ Messenger ช่วยปิดการขาย: Hyundai Brasil ได้ conversion สูงกว่าตาม email/โทร 3.5 เท่า. ส่วนความเร็วในการตอบ งานวิจัย DAS (2025) พบว่าดีลเลอร์ US 61% ตอบภายใน 15 นาที และ 19% ช้ากว่า 1 ชม. ใน Brand Buffet (มี.ค. 2026) Meta ระบุว่าธุรกิจขายรถเป็นกลุ่มที่ใช้แชทขายมากที่สุดในไทย

### Cited Findings

**Automotive Inventory Ads (AIA) / vehicle catalog**
- To promote automotive inventory you need an automotive (Vehicles) catalog, created by data feed or upload in Business/Commerce Manager. Field references are split into "Inventory Ads, Vehicle", "Inventory Ads, Dealership" and "Inventory Offers". Formats: Inventory Ads and Inventory Offer Ads; feeds in CSV/TSV/XML, one or many (e.g., per dealership). Meta pre-creates a vehicle set with all vehicles — [Meta Auto Ads catalog guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/auto-ads/guides/catalog) (search summary says updated 4 Dec 2025; the page I fetched showed no date)
- AIA advertise at the inventory/VIN level and retarget using engagement and the Pixel. Clicks go to a vehicle detail page on Meta or the dealer site. Product Level Video has been supported since Jan 2024 — [Phyron](https://www.phyron.com/insights/meta-auto-inventory-ads) (vendor); [AutoRevo](https://autorevo.com/meta-dynamic-inventory-ads-from-autorevo/) (vendor)
- **Marketplace**: "Starting January 30, 2023, Meta will no longer support the ability for sellers to create vehicle listings using a Facebook business page", along with deprecating the Vehicles tab and the Manage Inventory tab. Dealers need paid AIA, typically via a Meta partner. Free person-to-person listings remain — [AutoRevo](https://autorevo.com/meta-dynamic-inventory-ads-from-autorevo/); [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/facebook-automotive-brands/amp)
- Operational pain: missing product IDs, inconsistent inventory updates, and ads for sold cars. One used-car group with about 25,000 vehicles aimed to never show ads for sold or unavailable cars — [BuyerBridge AIA 2.0](https://support.buyerbridge.com/knowledge/aia-2.0-the-essential-guide-for-automotive-digital-agencies); [Dataiads By My Car case](https://www.dataiads.io/en/case-studies/by-my-car)

**Messenger in automotive sales (case studies)**
- **Hyundai Brasil + Smarters**: a Messenger assistant with quick-reply guided forms, and click-to-Messenger ads tested against website-assistant ads over a 2-week A/B. Versus email/phone follow-up: **4X higher sales rate, 3.5X higher conversion rate, 13% lower cost per lead** (partnership from early 2019; test dates not given) — [Meta success story: Hyundai](https://developers.facebook.com/success-stories/hyundai/)
- **Grupo Savol (Brazil dealer group)**: CTM ads to recent buyers for service bookings. Over 10,000 appointments booked via Messenger (2018–2020) and +10% average monthly bookings — [Meta success story: Grupo Savol](https://developers.facebook.com/success-stories/grupo-savol/)
- Thailand (non-auto): FWD Thailand with partner AiChat built a Messenger quote flow promoted by CTM ads, targeting 5X quote conversion — [AiChat case](https://aichat.com/?p=7791)
- Thailand: Meta told Thai media that car dealers are among the sectors that use chat most for selling (Business AI launch, Mar 2026) — [Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/03/meta-launchs-business-ai-via-messenger-for-thai-sme/)

**Response-time and chat-conversion data**
- DAS Technology study (2025 NADA Show; 1,700 US dealerships, Q3–Q4 2024): **61% responded within 15 minutes** (up from 55%), **19% took over an hour** (down from 33% in 2023), and 74% included no price quote — [Dealership Guy](https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/study-finds-many-dealers-fall-short-on-key-lead-processes-2025-01-28); [Auto Remarketing](https://autoremarketing.com/trends/study-dealership-online-lead-response-time-often-falls-short) (US, web leads, not Messenger-specific)
- Older benchmarks (dates unclear, some vendor-commissioned): 49% of dealerships responded within 5 minutes (Conversica mystery shop); shoppers answered within 10 minutes were 3x more likely to visit (Digital Air Strike); average response 4.7 hours (ResponseLogix/Capgemini) — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/analysis/study-car-dealers-are-best-effective-lead-follow/); [WardsAuto](https://www.wardsauto.com/news/archive-wards-study-says-dealers-slow-to-respond/776925/); [F&I Magazine](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/study-shows-dealers-responses-to-internet-leads-lags-consumer-expectations) (mapping of each stat to each outlet comes from a search summary; verify before quoting)
- Fullpath 2026 report (425,000 messages, 776 North American dealers; **website AI chat, not Messenger**): once a shopper sends a 6th message, conversion probability rises to 45.6% — [Fullpath](https://www.fullpath.com/impact-report-ai-chat-for-dealers-in-2026/)
- Dealer forum skepticism about a claimed 40% chat-to-lead rate on Facebook Marketplace — [DealerRefresh](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/goto/post?id=54251)
- Meta global claims for Thailand: CTM ads have a median 20% lower cost per purchase (28 A/B tests), and Kantar reports "55% increase in converting leads into sales" for businesses using messaging — [The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/24/07/2025/158757)

### Inferences
- For a Thai used-car dealer, the Meta-native "ideal loop" would be: vehicle catalog → AIA / CTM ads → Messenger chat (`referral.ad_id` and `ads_context_data` identify the car or ad) → in-house CRM qualifies and sells → CAPI for Business Messaging sends QualifiedLead/Purchase back. Oxlet's stock system could feed the vehicle catalog. Whether AIA is available in Thailand is unconfirmed (see Gaps).
- Speed-to-lead evidence (minutes matter) supports Oxlet's SLA clock. Meta echoes let it measure real human response time even when staff reply from Business Suite.
- The published Messenger-specific automotive results (Hyundai, Savol) are Meta-published, old (2019–2020) and Brazilian. They are directional only.

### Gaps
- **Country availability of Automotive Inventory Ads in Thailand was not found.** Meta's catalog page lists no countries, and the vendor sources are US/EU-centric.
- No Thai or SEA dealer case study found with Messenger response-time or conversion metrics.
- No Meta data on Marketplace vehicle-listing chat volume for Thailand, or on whether paid Marketplace vehicle placement exists there.
- No independent (non-vendor) study isolating Messenger chat conversion for car sales.
